# Create a PNG or JPEG cover from video

Compare cover candidates on iPhone or iPad, or step through the original video on Mac. Choose one still, crop it and export a PNG or JPEG thumbnail.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6508171056)

[Read help](https://digit-video.luopeike.com/help)

## Use Cover mode

Cover mode is designed for fewer, stronger stills. It helps you avoid scanning the full timeline manually when you only need one or two representative images.

## Look for readable composition

Choose frames where the subject is centered, text is readable, and the scene is not between two actions.

## Export selected covers

Close the comparison and leave just one frame selected to crop a PNG or JPEG cover. For a PDF contact sheet, check the frame count and time range: it uses the selection, or all remaining results when nothing is selected (up to 120 per PDF).

## Which frame makes the better cover?

### iPhone / iPad

Select exactly two extracted stills and tap “Compare Two Frames” next to the selection count. Compare the expressions and millisecond timecodes. Closing the comparison leaves both images selected.

## Images you can put to work

Turn a product clip into listing images, keep tutorial steps in your notes, or collect stills for a presentation or reference board. Review the extracted frames, remove unwanted images and export the ones you choose. A contact sheet is a visual overview, not an automatically written tutorial or an editable video timeline.

## Export cover images and printable PDF contact sheets

On Mac, iPhone or iPad, select one result and choose Export → Export Cover. Choose the original ratio, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:3, adjust the crop position and maximum edge, then save PNG or JPEG. JPEG can be limited to 2 MB; if it cannot fit, choose smaller dimensions. Images are never upscaled. For a printable overview, choose Export → PDF Contact Sheet, A4 or Letter, and 2–4 columns. Up to 120 selected frames are ordered chronologically and paginated automatically, with the video name and timestamps. With no selection, all remaining results are used. A PNG contact sheet still accepts at most 40 frames.

## Which image and overview formats can I export?

Selected stills export as separate PNG files. Export Cover crops one frame to PNG or JPEG, with an optional 2 MB JPEG limit. A PNG contact sheet accepts up to 40 frames; an A4 or Letter PDF contact sheet accepts up to 120 with timestamps and automatic pagination. On Mac, batch automatic export additionally supports PNG/JPEG images and optional JSON metadata. Exporting does not save a project to History; use Save explicitly.
